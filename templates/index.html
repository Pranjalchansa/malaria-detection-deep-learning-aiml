About Malaria

Malaria is a life-threatening disease caused by parasites transmitted through infected mosquitoes.

How It Spreads

The parasite enters the bloodstream through mosquito bites and infects red blood cells.

Symptoms

  • High Fever
  • Chills
  • Headache
  • Vomiting
  • Fatigue

Severe Effects

Untreated malaria may lead to anemia, organ failure, coma or death.

Prevention

  • Mosquito Nets
  • Repellents
  • Clean Environment

How The AI Works

1️⃣ Image Upload

User uploads microscopic blood smear image.

2️⃣ Preprocessing

Image is resized and normalized using MobileNetV2 preprocessing.

3️⃣ Deep Learning Model

CNN extracts features from infected and uninfected cells.

4️⃣ Prediction

AI predicts malaria infection probability and provides recommendation.

Transfer Learning allows high accuracy using pretrained ImageNet weights.
